# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Internal energy auditors, building engineers, store managers, and SPARTA Energy system administrators monitoring Alfamart retail store energy and utility usage.

## Product Purpose
Real-time energy and utility monitoring system for Alfamart stores, providing IoT telemetry visualization, audit session tracking, hardware device management, and integration with the SPARTA Energy ecosystem.

## Positioning
Specialized retail store IoT telemetry and audit platform tailored specifically for Alfamart's multi-branch energy efficiency monitoring infrastructure.

## Operating Context
Used in retail energy management, hardware installation setup at store locations via ESP32 devices (`energy-meter.sparta-alfamart.web.id`), energy auditing workflows, and real-time monitoring of 3-phase electrical metrics (Voltage, Current, Active Power, Power Factor).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Multi-store overview gallery and status filtering (Live Audit, Historical Log, Unassigned).
- IoT session telemetry monitoring with 3-phase circular gauges and load trend charts.
- Direct external integration links to SPARTA Realtime Smart Energy Meter setup and SPARTA Energy Audit Platform.
- Future multi-utility expansion support (water flow rate, environment temperature/humidity) and AI-driven cost prediction/anomaly detection.

## Brand Commitments
SPARTA Energy and Alfamart branding (`/assets/Building-Logo.png`). Clean Light mode primary theme with dark mode extensibility using green/emerald/cyan accent colors (`#2c7a57`).

## Product Principles
- Seamless ecosystem integration with SPARTA Energy tools.
- Operational clarity for field technicians and energy auditors.
- High-visibility real-time IoT metrics and historical audit logs.
- Extensible UI architecture for multi-utility monitoring.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive design with clear visual indicator badges, accessible color contrast, keyboard shortcuts (e.g. Ctrl+B for sidebar toggle), and legible telemetry status dials.
